Birthdays keeps the birthdays of your friends, family and colleagues and reminds you in time. For each person you decide when you get reminded: on the day, one day, one week or two weeks before, or at your own interval. That leaves enough time for a present.

<b>Your data stays on your phone</b>
• No account, no cloud, no ads, no tracking
• The app has no internet access. Android itself prevents data from leaving the phone through the app.
• Android's automatic backup to Google's cloud is turned off.
• A backup is only created when you create it yourself.

<b>What the app does</b>
• Home screen with all birthdays by month, starting with the next one, with days left, the age they will turn and the star sign
• Whoever has a birthday today appears at the top in a card of their own
• Calendar with all birthdays of the month and an overview of the whole year
• Several reminders per person, each with a time and a short hint like "buy a present"
• Reminders as a notification or full screen like an alarm clock
• Default reminders for all entries, custom ones or none for single people
• Groups like family, friends and work, each with its own color and as a filter on the home screen
• Note and photo for every entry, the photo from the gallery, the camera or a contact
• Import birthdays from your contacts, as often as you like, without duplicates
• The year of birth is optional
• Search in names and notes, a month name shows all birthdays in that month
• On the lock screen show everything, only the name or no name
• Time zones: birthdays follow your time at home, even when you travel
• February 29 on February 28 or March 1 in other years
• Light, dark or like the phone
• English and German
• Save and load a backup, with pictures
