Birthdays are special days. With Birthdays, the birthdays of your family, friends and colleagues are always in view. The app reminds you in good time, exactly the way you want. On the day itself or days and weeks ahead: you decide when and how you want to be reminded.

<b>Your birthdays. Your data. Your phone.</b>
Privacy isn't a setting, it's a given.

• No account, no cloud, no ads and no tracking.
• The app needs no internet access. Android technically prevents it from sending any data over the internet.
• Android's automatic backup to Google's cloud is turned off as well.
• Your data stays on your device. A backup is only created when you decide to make one.

<b>Everything in view, nothing forgotten</b>

<b>Your personal birthday overview</b>
• All birthdays neatly sorted by month, starting with the next upcoming one.
• See at a glance how many days are left, how old someone is turning and which zodiac sign they have.
• Anyone celebrating today is highlighted.

<b>Calendar and reminders</b>
• Monthly calendar with all birthdays.
• Multiple reminders per person, each with its own time and a personal note, for example “Buy a gift”.
• Notification or full screen reminder in the style of an alarm clock.
• Set general reminders or decide for each person whether and when you want to be reminded.

<b>People who belong together</b>
• Organize your entries in groups such as Family, Friends or Work, each with its own color.
• Filter your birthday overview by the groups you care about right now.
• Add personal notes and a photo from your gallery, camera or contacts to any entry.
• Import birthdays from your address book as often as you like, without creating duplicates.

<b>Lots of small details that make the difference</b>
• The birth year is optional. A birthday works without an age, too.
• Search by name and notes, or type a month name to find all birthdays in that month.
• Decide what appears on the lock screen: all details, just the name or nothing at all.
• Birthdays follow your home time zone, even while you're traveling.
• For February 29, choose whether you're reminded on February 28 or March 1.
• Light theme, dark theme or automatic to match your phone.
• Fully available in English and German.

<b>Back up and restore</b>
• Create your own backups and restore them at any time, including the photos.
